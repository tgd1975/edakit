ADLSC, Atoomnet's Digital Led String Controller

Why: the WLED controller – the board makers build to drive addressable LED strips from an ESP32 at several amperes: a 5 V supply of 10 A, the ESP32's 3.3 V data shifted to 5 V by a 74AHCT125 with 100 Ω in series, the strips' power switched by the firmware and fused per output. No reference circuit has an LED-strip output, and of the open designs the Corne feeds its SK6812s one diode below 5 V to make 3.3 V data reach them and the SparkFun converter shifts levels with BSS138s; ADLSC brings the TTL-input buffer, a reverse-polarity N-MOSFET in the ground return, a voltage supervisor on the ESP32's EN, P-MOSFET high-side switches driven from a GPIO through an NPN, glass fuses with blown-fuse LEDs, a polyfuse, TVS diodes on every 5 V rail, and an ESP32 development board plugged in behind a Schottky diode – real parts with their datasheets, in a class of board that WLED's users copy, where a false alarm would meet many.

atoomnetmarc, ADLSC, Atoomnet's Digital Led String Controller ("ESP32 based 5V digital LED controller build for use with WLED"), https://github.com/atoomnetmarc/ADLSC at commit 3aecf18 (3aecf18cfb2dffa2b83f3e08038c563ee9e2df65, 2026-01-23), the schematic Kicad/ADLSC.kicad_sch (title block rev V1.0, 2025-01-05, KiCad 8.0.7; the same drawing as Kicad/ADLSC-schematic.pdf) and the repository's README.md (power supply, WLED configuration, wiring), read on 2026-11-03 from a read-only clone. The netlist was resolved from the schematic (wires, junctions, labels, power symbols) by a reader outside this repository and checked against the board file Kicad/ADLSC.kicad_pcb of the same commit: all 187 pads are on the same nets there. · SVG

With F1 at its 0.15 Ω and a typical dropout it works – a worst case, not the typical board.
simLIM.rangeU2real+5V sags to 4.23–4.49 V with the LEDs lit: the supply wires and Q1 lift GND by 0.2–0.3 V, F1 at its R1max drops 0.36 V – the 74AHCT125 leaves its 4.5 V minimum. The same +5V reaches the MAX809's own threshold (4.27–4.49 V): in the worst case the supervisor resets the ESP32 as the LEDs come on. The polyfuse in front of the 5 V logic is the cause.

edakit's false alarms on this design, fixed

  • The bidirectional SMBJ5.0CA across the terminal (D4) judged as one direction – reached by the reversed 5 V on both pins (PATH.reverse on D4.1, D4.2): the parts schema has values tvs "bidirectional" now, which PATH.reverse tolerates and LIM.v holds either way (TD-132).
  • The reverse-polarity N-MOSFET in the ground return (Q1) unknown to PATH.reverse, which walked from J1's pins as if GND were the return – 18 findings on the plus side and on Q1 itself (D1.2, D3.1, D5.1, Q1, Q2, Q4, R1.1, R2.1, R3.1, R19.1, U1.3, U2.14, C2.1): a low-side N-MOSFET is now a cut of catalog/paths.toml [reverse], and only the parts between the terminal's own nets count (TD-125).
  • The gate of the reverse-polarity N-MOSFET in the ground return, tied straight to the net the supply comes in on (Q1 on +5V_IN), taken as floating (LVL.undefined on Q1).
