SparkFun Logic Level Converter

Why: the BSS138 level shifter sits on many maker boards that join 3.3 V and 5 V parts. It brings a circuit whose function is a level in both directions – each channel simulated, driven from either side –, which no reference circuit has, with real MOSFETs from their datasheet.

SparkFun Electronics, schematic Logic_Level_Bidirectional, design Patrick Alberts, Rev V01, dated 2013-09-26 – https://cdn.sparkfun.com/datasheets/BreakoutBoards/Logic_Level_Bidirectional.pdf (linked from the product page https://www.sparkfun.com/sparkfun-logic-level-converter-bi-directional.html), read on 2026-10-31.
