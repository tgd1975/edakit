edakit documentation

ESPurna-H

Why: the only board here with mains on it – an HLK-PM01 behind a thermal fuse, a 500 mA fuse and a varistor, a 10 A relay that switches the load, and an HLW8012 power monitor on a 1 mΩ shunt and a 5 × 470 kΩ divider from neutral, whose 0 Ω resistor ties the low-voltage ground to the line. No reference circuit (tests/reference.py has none with mains or a relay) and no other open design brings LIM.hazardous and the domain mains onto a real board, nor a relay coil driven from a 3.3 V GPIO with its flyback diode; around them sit the low-voltage checks edakit does make – the 2N7002's gate drive, the regulator, the ESP8266's levels and boot pins. An HLK-PM01, an ESP module and a relay is the common recipe of a homemade mains smart switch, and ESPurna is a widely used open firmware for such switches, so a false alarm here would meet many.

Xose Pérez (tinkerman.cat), ESPurna-H, an ESP8266 wall switch with an HLK-PM01 and a power monitor, https://github.com/xoseperez/espurna-board at commit f433874 (f43387465bcd7fee6e2e6ac7c699a5177faa2fcc, 2018-04-16), the Eagle schematic espurna-h/v0.8/espurna-h.sch (title block "ESPURNA-H", version 0.8.20171028; the same file as espurna-h/espurna-h.sch), read on 2026-11-03 from a read-only clone. The bill of materials is the repository's README. The netlist was resolved from the schematic's XML by a reader outside this repository and checked against the board file espurna-h/v0.8/espurna-h.brd: all 142 pads of the schematic's 39 nets are on the same signals there. · SVG

checkDOCS.fileedakit.tomlcriterionThe transcription has no requirements file: what the published design must do stands in its own documents, not in edakit's form. edakit requires the file before a release (handbook 3.6); it is no point of the design.
checkLIM.hazardousNEUTRALrealMains neutral against GND – and GND is the line here (R42): the full mains voltage, 358 V peak at 230 V + 10 %. Recognised, not checked, as edakit means it. The board is meant to sit behind a wall switch in the wall box; the ESPurna README warns never to connect the board's ground to a computer and never to flash it while it is on mains.
checkLIM.hazardousNOrealThe load's line at the terminal JP42: at GND (the line) while the relay is on, pulled to neutral through the load while it is off – hazardous voltage, recognised and not checked.
checkLIM.hazardousNCrealAs NO, the other way round: the changeover contact brought out to JP42.2 carries mains whenever the relay leaves it open.
checkLIM.hazardousV2P_D1realThe first tap of the 5 × 470 kΩ divider from neutral to the HLW8012's V2P: four fifths of the mains peak, 286 V. Hazardous voltage on the board, recognised and not checked – its spacing to the low-voltage side is the reviewers'.
checkLIM.hazardousV2P_D2realThe divider's second tap, 215 V peak: hazardous voltage on the board, recognised and not checked.
checkLIM.hazardousV2P_D3realThe divider's third tap, 143 V peak: hazardous voltage on the board, recognised and not checked.
checkLIM.hazardousV2P_D4realThe divider's last tap before V2P, 72 V peak – still above ES1's 60 V. Only V2P itself (0.15 V across R50's 1 kΩ) is below it.
checkLIM.vJP2known gapThe KF301-5.0 is rated 300 V AC rms (UL; its body is marked 300VAC); 230 V + 10 % is 253 V rms, within it. edakit knows no AC net: it holds the declared peak, 358 V, against the rating as if both were DC. The fix needs AC ratings and AC nets (an rms value beside the peak) – no small change; mains stays unchecked by decision 7.
checkLIM.vJP42known gapAs JP2: 253 V rms at the line and load terminals against the KF301's 300 V rms rating fits; edakit compares the 358 V peak with the rms rating.
checkLIM.vR51known gapedakit holds a resistor to the highest voltage of its nets against GND (358 V at neutral), not to the voltage across it: the five 470 kΩ of the chain share the mains, 72 V peak (51 V rms) each, well inside the 0805's 150 V working voltage (DC or rms). A declared range cannot say that the chain's taps move together; the voltage across needs the simulation of an AC source or AC nets – no small change.
checkLIM.vR52known gapAs R51: its tap V2P_D1 reaches 286 V against GND, the resistor itself sees a fifth of the mains, 72 V peak.
checkLIM.vR53known gapAs R51: its tap V2P_D2 reaches 215 V against GND, the resistor itself 72 V peak. R54 and R55 (143 V and 72 V at their taps) pass by the same reading.
checkLIM.vU$1.2known gapThe HLK-PM01's AC input is rated 85 … 265 V AC rms or 120 … 350 V DC; its parts entry can only state the DC range as the pin's limit. edakit holds neutral's ±358 V peak against ±350 V, while 253 V rms is within the module's AC range (375 V peak). AC ratings have no field – no small change.
checkLVL.undefinedQ4realQ4's gate hangs on GPIO5 alone, without a pull-down: until the firmware drives GPIO5, SEL is undefined and CF1 may give either quantity. Harmless – the firmware sets SEL before it reads CF1 – but the gate floats, unlike Q3's with R32.
checkPATH.unfedU$1.1known gapThe module's AC input is fed from the mains terminals JP42 (line, through F1 and F2) and JP2 (neutral). edakit knows no mains supply: a connector feeds a net only with a vbus role, a battery or a simulation source (catalog/paths.toml [supply]), or marked reversible – which sends PATH.reverse through the whole mains side. A supply role for a mains terminal would close it.
checkPATH.unfedU$1.2known gapAs U$1.1, on neutral: fed by the terminal JP2, which edakit cannot name as a mains supply.
checkREQ.missing/leaveGPIO4realThe external button's wire (JP22.3) runs straight into GPIO4 – R22 is a pull-up, not in series; a discharge or a short at the wall switch reaches the ESP's pin.
checkREQ.value/pinIC1.4 (VOUT)realThe AMS1117 datasheet asks for 22 µF (solid tantalum) at the output for stability; the ESPurna-H has 10 µF tantalum (C12) and 100 nF (C21) – 10.1 µF.
simLIM.rangeQ3realGPIO12 drives the relay's 2N7002 through R31/R32 at 3.0 V; its RDS(on) is specified at 5 V and 10 V only, its threshold up to 2.5 V. The model – fitted to the typical part, run at the highest threshold – still pulls the relay in (4.65 V on the coil, 3.75 V needed), but the datasheet does not promise the 71 mA coil current at 3 V: a logic-level MOSFET would.
simLIM.rangeQ4known gapLIM.range/gate (CK-4) finds Q4 half on at VGS 3.3 V. It only pulls SEL low through R49 (0.5 mA): even the part at its highest threshold holds SEL below 1 mV. The rule does not weigh the current, so it warns where nothing burns – as on the SparkFun converter's BSS138.

edakit's false alarms on this design, fixed

  • A need on a regulator's VOUT pin left open while its tab, the same terminal, carries the output: taken as not connected (REQ.missing on IC1.2) instead of judged on the output (REQ.value on IC1.4).
