edakit documentation

Published open designs

Real designs, transcribed from their published schematics into edakit projects (tests/open_designs/, maker-coverage FT-21), with parts entries from the parts' datasheets. Every finding of edakit check and edakit sim is judged: real – a point of the design, lacks – a fact the transcription or the datasheets lack, criterion – edakit's yardstick, not the design's, known gap – edakit judges it wrong, not fixed yet. The false alarms the designs showed are fixed (tests/test_open_designs.py, tests/test_open_design_fixes.py).
