GuitarML Tubescreamer/Rat combo

Why: two drive circuits makers copy often, a Tube Screamer-style and a Rat-style stage, on one board: op-amp stages biased at half the supply, BJT and JFET followers, a 3PDT switch on the board that orders the effects, and footswitches wired by hand. It brings real op-amps where the reference circuit guitar has a test entry, and a reversed electrolytic (C19) that edakit finds.

Keith Bloemer (GuitarML), GuitarPedalPCBs, https://github.com/GuitarML/GuitarPedalPCBs at commit e2fa7e1 (e2fa7e1e22f4939215cfbb1d5a3df2a66068275e, 2026-08-05, "Update README.md"), the pedal TS_Rat_Combo_1590bb ("Custom Tubescreamer/Rat Combo with order switch", the repository's README; the board's silkscreen "Distortion Drive v1.0 9/3/2025 GuitarML"), the schematic TS_Rat_Combo_1590bb/jack_pedal.kicad_sch (KiCad 9), read on 2026-10-31 from a read-only clone (the files' git blob hashes are those of the commit). The netlist was resolved from the schematic (wires, labels, power symbols, the instances of project jack_pedal) by a reader outside this repository and checked against the board file TS_Rat_Combo_1590bb/jack_pedal.kicad_pcb of the same commit, the footprints matched to the symbols by their uuid: all 182 pins of the sheet are on the same nets there (the board's references of the wire pads drop the schematic's numbers – In1 is In, InG3 is OutG, InG5 is InG, both swGnd pads are swGnd). · SVG

simLIM.rangeU2realThe LM301A runs from VCC, 8.73 V behind the 1N5817 – below the 10 V (±5 V) from which its data sheet specifies it (SNOSBS0D, note 1 of the electrical characteristics). Rats run on 9 V in practice; the data sheet promises nothing there – offset, swing and common-mode range are unspecified. The TL072 beside it is specified from 4.5 V.
simLIM.vC19realC19 (10 µF electrolytic, the TS output coupling) sits with its plus – pad 1 of the CP_Radial_D5.0mm footprint, the square pad with the '+' – at ts_out, held at 0 V by R27, and its minus at R26 from Q2's emitter, 3.0–3.4 V: reverse-biased in every state. The schematic draws an unpolarized Device:C, the footprint is polarized. Turned round, the finding goes (tests/test_open_designs.py).

edakit's false alarms on this design, fixed

  • A base biased by resistors – the TS's 2SC1815 followers through 510 kΩ from the divider node VREF – judged as floating while 'the firmware is not running' (LVL.undefined): only a resistor to the emitter or to a net a pin types counted, not ground and not a divider's middle.
  • A coupling capacitor in a signal path judged from extreme to extreme of its two nets' ranges – C10 and C19, 8.5 V on one side against −4.5 V on the other, 13 V × 1.5 above their 16 V (LIM.v); its two sides carry the same signal, so like ends are compared.
  • Several wires of one net between two boards – the ground to the DC jack, both jack sleeves and both footswitches – stopped the simulation (SIM.converge): the second cable of a net found its ends on different nets.
  • The order switch and the footswitches were never judged by REQ.value/switch: their nets run through cables, and the simulation's per-board node names ('IN__main') were looked up among the signal nets.
  • A switch of three poles could not be described: the roles of the second and third pole were unknown, the simulation switched only the first, and REQ.value/switch took all nine lugs for one contact.
