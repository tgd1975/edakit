# Inside edakit

For developers and for anyone who wants to see what steers edakit, not only what it does. The sources below are shown
on the site as they are in the repository, each page marked as a source. Read them as such: the guides say what
edakit does, and a change to a source is a piece of work of its own ([organisation](organisation.md)).

- [Concepts](concepts/) – how a part of edakit should work, written before it is built and kept as the record of what
  was decided: what is open, what is decided and built, what was discarded.
- [Skills](../skills/) – the plugin's workflows, one prompt each, called as `/edakit:<name>` in a session.
- [Agents](../agents/) – the roles that run in a context of their own, such as the reviewers.
- [Evals](../evals/README.md) – the tasks and graders that measure whether the skills do what their pages say.
- [Rules of a session](../CLAUDE.md) – what a session in the edakit repository itself follows.

The search finds every page of them.
