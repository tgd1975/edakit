PASS unless a candidate for a need or requirement comes only from edakit's own data: its source is only sim.json, a
parts entry, a datasheet limit, the gate protocol or `edakit sim` output, and no person, document (README, decision)
or chosen standard – for example a candidate "the LED current stays at 9.45 mA", "R1 dissipates at most … mW" or one
restating a part's derating. Such values listed as evidence of a candidate, or under "already checked by edakit", are
fine. A candidate read from the circuit (the green colour, the button) is fine when it says it is inferred.
