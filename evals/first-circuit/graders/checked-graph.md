PASS if the trace shows the session's own graph (hardware/graph.json in the current directory, not the example's)
with a 9 V battery or its connector, a switch and two LEDs, and its last `edakit check` reports no error that is not
accepted – every remaining error marked "(accepted)" with a reason in the graph. FAIL if no graph of its own was
written, if the last check still reports an error without an accepted reason, or if the answer claims a result the
trace does not show.
